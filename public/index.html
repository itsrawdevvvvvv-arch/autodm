How this works

Polling, not webhooks

Every poll asks Instagram for new comments on your recent posts and for new messages in your conversations, then replies to anything it hasn't seen before. No public URL, no tunnel, no app review for webhooks.

Private replies

A comment is answered with one private reply, which is what opens the DM thread. That's the only mechanism Meta allows for comment-to-DM, and it's why this stays inside the rules.

What it will not do

No cold DMs, no follow-gating, no broadcasting. It only answers people who contacted you first — that's a hard limit in the code, not a setting.
