01 — capture

Comment triggers that actually match how people type.

People comment link, LINK!!, send it, price? or a single emoji. Viraloop matches keywords case-insensitively, accepts several spellings per trigger, and supports a catch-all for everything else.

  • Multiple keywords per trigger, per post
  • Separate replies for different posts and reels
  • Optional reply to the public comment as well as the DM

02 — stories

Story replies, finally out of the void.

Story replies are the warmest messages you get and the easiest to lose. Viraloop picks them up, tags them against the story that produced them, and can hand back the right link straight away.

  • Works with story replies and sticker taps
  • Every reply is attributed to the story it came from
  • Auto-reply, or queue it for you to answer

03 — nuture

Follow-ups with manners.

A gentle nudge twelve hours after a link was sent recovers conversations that would otherwise go quiet. Viraloop schedules it inside Instagram's messaging window and stops the moment someone replies.

  • Configurable delay: minutes, hours, or next day
  • Sequence stops automatically on any reply
  • Hard limit of one follow-up per conversation, by default
