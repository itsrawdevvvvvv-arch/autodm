Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026. Written to describe what this product actually does — not a template.

In short: this website sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts. The product stores the account details, automation settings and message logs needed to reply on your behalf, and nothing else. We don't sell data and we don't train models on your messages.

1. This website

These marketing pages are static files. They don't set cookies, don't use analytics, and don't include third-party scripts, pixels or fonts served from another domain. Forms on this site open your own email client with the message pre-filled — the contents never pass through our servers.

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log standard request information (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for security and operational purposes. That logging is outside our control; see GitHub's privacy statement for details.

2. What the product collects

When you create a workspace and connect Instagram, we process:

  • Account data — name, email address, workspace name.
  • Instagram account data — the professional account ID, username and the access token issued by Meta.
  • Automation configuration — the posts, keywords and message templates you create.
  • Message data — the comments, story replies and DMs your automations act on, plus the automated messages sent, with timestamps and delivery status.
  • Billing data — handled by our payment processor. We never see or store card numbers.

We don't collect data about your followers beyond the messages needed to reply to them.

3. Why we process it

  • To run the automations you configured (performance of our contract with you).
  • To provide support and answer your emails (legitimate interest).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and debug failures (legitimate interest).
  • To meet tax and accounting obligations (legal obligation).

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of processors, each bound by a data-processing agreement:

  • Meta Platforms — the Instagram messaging API, which is how messages are sent and received.
  • Our cloud hosting and database provider — application hosting and encrypted storage.
  • Our email provider — transactional email such as invites, receipts and security notices.
  • Our payment processor — subscription billing, once paid plans begin.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't share it for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

  • Message logs: 30 days on Free, 12 months on paid plans, then deleted.
  • Account and automation data: while your account is active.
  • After you close your account: deleted within 30 days, except invoice records we must keep for tax purposes.

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — and for EU/UK visitors, under GDPR — you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, withdraw consent, or object to processing. Email hello@viraloop.co and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or your local supervisory authority in the EU/UK.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access tokens are stored encrypted and scoped to messaging permissions only. Access to production data is limited to the engineers who need it, and is logged. If a breach affects your data, we'll notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

8. Children

The service isn't intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly process data of children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll email account holders at least 14 days before the change takes effect and update the date at the top of this page.
