The gap

A comment is the highest-intent message you'll ever get.

Someone who comments “link?” on your reel is telling you exactly what they want, right now. Reply nine hours later and you're a stranger again. Reply in ten seconds and you're the obvious choice.

Most creators lose this race because the inbox is manual, the day is full, and DMs don't wait. That's the whole reason Viraloop exists: the reply happens at the speed of the comment, not the speed of your calendar.

What it does

One automation engine, six moving parts.

Build a flow once and it runs on every post, reel and story — with your words, your links, your tone.

Comment triggers

Match keywords on any post or reel and send the right DM instantly. One keyword can route to a link, a price list, a booking page or a whole sequence.

Story reply capture

Turn story replies and sticker taps into a tracked conversation instead of an unread inbox item you'll never find again.

Timed follow-ups

Nudge a quiet lead minutes or hours later, inside the window Instagram allows. No spam, no cold DMs — just a second chance at the conversation.

Link delivery

Send a clean, trackable link — a resource, a checkout, a form, a call booking — and see which post actually produced the click.

Creator storefront

A single page for your digital products, with checkout built in. Your DMs and your store finally share the same link.

Human handoff

When a conversation needs you, the automation stops and hands it over with the full context attached. You never talk to someone twice about the same thing.

Why we're careful

Policy-safe by design.

Half the DM automation advice online will get your account restricted. Requiring a follow before unlocking a link, blasting cold DMs to scraped lists, faking human replies — Meta's rules are clear that none of that is allowed, and the enforcement lands on you, not on the tool that suggested it.

Viraloop only builds what the official API supports. That makes us slightly less flashy and considerably less likely to cost you your account.

  • No follow-gating. We never require someone to follow you to receive a link. Meta prohibits it and we won't build it.
  • No cold DMs. We only reply to people who messaged or commented first.
  • Official API only. Meta-approved scopes, revocable any time from your Instagram settings.
  • You can see everything. Every automated message is logged with the trigger that caused it.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Email hello@viraloop.co — a human answers, usually within a day.

Will this get my Instagram account banned?
No. Viraloop only uses Meta's official Instagram messaging API with approved scopes, and we deliberately do not support follow-gating, cold DMs or any pattern Meta's platform rules prohibit. If a feature can't be built within the rules, we don't ship it.
Do I need a business or creator account?
Yes — automation requires an Instagram professional account (Creator or Business) linked to a Facebook page. Switching is free and reversible inside Instagram settings.
Does it reply to every comment?
Only the ones you ask it to. You choose the keywords per post, and anything that doesn't match stays in your normal inbox for you to answer yourself.
What happens to my data?
We store your account details, automation settings and message logs so the product can work. We don't sell data, we don't run advertising pixels on this site, and you can delete everything by disconnecting the account or emailing us. The privacy policy lists exactly what's processed.
Is it really free?
Yes — no plan, no card, no trial. Viraloop is software you run yourself for a single Instagram account, and the Cloudflare Workers free plan costs nothing.
What's the catch?
There isn't one. It runs on your own server, so there is no bill to send you. If you later want it hosted for you with more accounts, that would be a separate conversation — nothing on this page changes.
