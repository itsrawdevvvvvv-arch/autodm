About

We think the inbox is the most under-used surface on the internet.

Viraloop is a small, independent product built for one narrow job: making sure the people who raise their hand in your comments hear back from you before they lose interest.

Why we built it

Instagram rewards posting and punishes follow-through. You publish a reel, three hundred people comment “link”, and the only tool you have for answering them is your thumb.

We built Viraloop because the gap between “someone is interested” and “someone got the link” is where most creator revenue quietly dies. Not for lack of audience — for lack of a reply.

How we work

We're a small team, we answer our own support email, and we ship on a weekly rhythm. There is no sales team and no enterprise tier to negotiate — the pricing page is the whole menu.

If a feature can't be built inside Meta's platform rules, we don't build it. That costs us some growth-hack features that competitors ship, and it's the trade we've chosen.

Principles

Four rules we don't bend.

Your account comes first

Official API scopes, no password sharing, no grey-area automation. If we're ever unsure whether something is allowed, we assume it isn't.

No dark patterns

No fake scarcity, no invented testimonials, no “only 3 spots left” timers. If we quote a number it will be one we can show you.

Humans stay in the loop

Automation should handle the first reply, not the relationship. Handoff is one click, and the automation never talks over you.

You own your data

Export your automations and logs any time. Disconnect and everything is deleted within 30 days. No lock-in, no hostage data.

Under the hood

Boring infrastructure, on purpose.

The product runs on Meta's official Instagram messaging API, with message processing in the EU/India regions and encrypted storage. This website is a static site — no analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, no cookie banner because there's nothing to consent to.

  • Instagram messaging API — approved scopes only
  • Messages encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access revocable from Instagram settings, any time
  • No third-party trackers on this site
