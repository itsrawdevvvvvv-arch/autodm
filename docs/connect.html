Connect

Connect your Instagram.

One button, the standard Instagram permission screen, thirty seconds. Your password never touches Viraloop — you approve specific permissions and can revoke them from Instagram at any time.

Setup needed

This site isn't linked to a running app yet

The Connect button needs a deployed instance of the app to receive Instagram's callback. Two minutes of setup, then this page becomes a one-click connect.

  1. Deploy the app — one click on Render, or npm start locally.
  2. Add your Meta app ID and secret to that server's environment.
  3. Register the redirect URI https://your-app/auth/callback in the Meta dashboard.
  4. Set APP_URL in the site generator so this button points at your app.

What the flow looks like

1

You press Connect

You're sent to instagram.com/oauth/authorize with our app ID and the three scopes we need.

2

You approve

Instagram shows its own permission screen — the same one every Meta app uses. You can decline any scope.

3

You land back connected

We exchange the one-time code for a long-lived token, store it on your server, and start polling for comments and messages.

Permissions

Exactly three, and why.

These are the only scopes Viraloop requests. Nothing about your password, your followers list or your other accounts.

Permissions Viraloop requests and what each is used for
ScopeWhat it allowsWhy Viraloop needs it
instagram_business_basicYour profile: username, account type, media listTo show which account is connected and to find your posts and reels
instagram_business_manage_messagesRead and send direct messagesTo send the reply when someone comments or messages you
instagram_business_manage_commentsRead comments on your mediaTo spot the comments that match your keywords

Revoke everything in two clicks: Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites → Viraloop → Remove. Automations stop immediately.

Before you start

Two things Instagram requires.

  • A Creator or Business account. Personal accounts can't use the messaging API. Switching is free and reversible in Instagram settings.
  • A Meta app with the Instagram product added. Your own app is enough — you don't need app review for your own account.

If you'd rather not go through OAuth at all, the dashboard also accepts a long-lived access token pasted directly. Same result, fewer screens.

No password, ever

Viraloop never sees your Instagram password. It receives a token issued by Meta, scoped to the permissions above, stored only on the server you deployed. Delete the server and the token is gone.

This is the same authorisation model every legitimate Instagram tool uses — and the opposite of tools that ask for your login.

FAQ

When the button misbehaves.

Still unsure? Email hello@viraloop.co — a human answers, usually within a day.

The permission screen says the app is in development mode.
That's expected for your own Meta app. Add your Instagram account as a tester under App roles, then connect — no app review is needed to use it yourself.
I get “Invalid redirect URI” after approving.
The URL in Meta's Valid OAuth Redirect URIs must match the server's PUBLIC_URL exactly, including https and the /auth/callback path. Meta rejects http URLs for anything other than localhost.
It says “Insufficient permissions” when reading comments.
The token was issued without instagram_business_manage_comments. Disconnect, then reconnect and approve every scope on the screen.
Can I connect more than one account?
One account per deployment. Connect a different account and the previous token is replaced.
How do I disconnect?
Dashboard → Account → Disconnect. That deletes the stored token. To revoke at Meta's side too, remove the app from Instagram's Apps and websites settings.
