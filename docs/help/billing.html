Access, cost and limits

3 min read · Updated 29 October 2026

What it costs

Nothing. There is no subscription, no per-message charge and no paid tier. Viraloop is a single-account tool you run yourself, so there is no bill for us to send you.

The one optional cost is hosting. The free tier of Render runs it fine; a free instance sleeps when idle, so a reply can be delayed by a minute or two until it wakes. A paid always-on instance removes that delay, but the software is identical.

One account per deployment

Each deployment connects to one Instagram account. That keeps the token, the stored data and the blast radius small. A second account means a second deployment — which on a free host is a second free service.

The access token

  • Long-lived Instagram tokens last about 60 days.
  • Refresh from the dashboard: Account → Refresh token.
  • Revoke any time from Instagram's Apps and websites settings.
  • The token is stored on your server only, in a file you control.

Limits

There is an hourly safety cap (default 200 replies) that exists to stop a runaway loop, not to meter you. Raise or lower it in Settings. Instagram applies its own per-account rate limits on top, which is why very high-volume posts can queue.

Getting your data out

Everything lives in JSON files under data/ on your server: rules, settings, activity and the account. Copy them, edit them or delete them. Deleting the folder is a complete wipe.
