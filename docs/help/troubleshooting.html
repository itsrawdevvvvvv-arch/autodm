Nothing is sending — a checklist

4 min read · Updated 29 October 2026

Work through these in order. In our experience the first two resolve most cases.

1. Is the automation live?

Draft automations don't send. Check the status pill on the automation — it should read Live, not Paused or Draft.

2. Do the keywords match the comment?

Open the log and look at recent comments. Matching is substring-based, so price matches “price list” but not “cost”. If the log shows comments with no trigger, add those phrases.

3. Is the post the right one?

Triggers are attached to specific posts and reels. A common mistake is building an automation for a reel, then testing on a carousel.

4. Has the account connection expired?

Meta connections can lapse if permissions are revoked or a password changes. Settings → Accounts shows a warning when this happens; reconnecting takes a minute.

5. Have you hit a limit?

Check usage against your plan allowance. If you're at 100%, automations pause and the queue waits rather than dropping messages. See message limits.

Still stuck?

Email hello@viraloop.co with your account email, the automation name and the time of a comment that should have triggered. Include the comment text if you have it — that's usually enough for us to find the cause in the log.
