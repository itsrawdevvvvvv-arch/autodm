Message limits, follow-ups and Instagram's messaging window

4 min read · Updated 29 October 2026

Automation on Instagram works inside a conversation that's already underway. That shapes everything about limits and timing.

The messaging window

Instagram's messaging API is built around a recent interaction. Automated messages are expected to follow promptly from the user's last comment or message. Viraloop schedules follow-ups inside that window and refuses to send outside it — even if you configure a longer delay.

Follow-up policy

  • One follow-up per conversation, by default.
  • The sequence stops immediately if the person replies.
  • Follow-ups never send to someone who already completed your target action.

Plan quotas

Automated DMs are counted per send against your monthly plan allowance: 1,000 on Free, 10,000 on Creator, 50,000 on Agency. Manual replies you type yourself are never counted.

At 80% of your allowance we email you. At 100%, automations pause — they don't silently drop messages. Nothing is lost; the queue resumes when the month resets or you upgrade.

Rate limits

Instagram applies its own per-account rate limits on top of our quotas. If you post something that goes very wide, some replies may be queued rather than sent instantly. The log shows queue time for every message.
