Connect your Instagram account

4 min read · Updated 29 October 2026

Viraloop connects through Meta's official Instagram messaging API. That means an approval flow rather than a password, and a connection you can revoke at any time.

What you need

  • An Instagram Creator or Business account. Personal accounts can't use the messaging API.
  • A Facebook page linked to the Instagram account.
  • Admin access to both.

Connecting

  1. In Viraloop, open Settings → Accounts → Connect Instagram.
  2. Approve the permissions shown. We ask for messaging access and basic profile information — nothing else.
  3. Choose the Instagram account and the linked Facebook page when prompted.
  4. You'll return to Viraloop with the account connected.

Revoking access

You can disconnect from Viraloop's settings, or from Instagram's own Apps and websites screen. Disconnecting stops all automations immediately and deletes stored message logs within 30 days.

Common errors

  • “No professional account found.” The account is still personal. Switch it in Instagram: Settings → Account type.
  • “Page not linked.” Link a Facebook page to the Instagram account, then retry.
  • Permission screen loops. Usually a cookie or third-party blocker. Try a private window with extensions disabled.
  • Connected, but nothing sends. See troubleshooting.
