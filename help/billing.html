Billing, beta access and cancellation

3 min read · Updated 29 October 2026

During the beta

Everything is free, no card is required, and nothing auto-charges. When paid plans begin, beta accounts get six months of the Creator plan free from that date.

After the beta

  • Creator — ₹499/month, one Instagram account, 10,000 automated DMs.
  • Agency — ₹1,499/month, three accounts, 50,000 automated DMs, three seats.

Prices are in INR and exclusive of GST. Indian customers see GST added at checkout; enter a GSTIN for input credit. International cards are charged in INR and converted by your bank.

Cancelling

Billing settings → Cancel. Automations stop at the end of the paid period, your data stays available for 30 days, then it's deleted. No cancellation call, no retention offer.

Refunds

Email hello@viraloop.co within 14 days of a charge that went wrong and we'll refund it. Include your account email and the invoice number.

Invoices

Every payment produces a GST invoice, downloadable from billing settings and emailed to the address on the account.
